# Simple Instructions

## Step 1: Run SQL (1 minute)

1. Open: https://supabase.com/dashboard/project/fpqmnfunfpkvdrxfazgj/editor
2. Click "New Query"
3. Open file: `ONE_COMPLETE_SETUP.sql`
4. Copy everything (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C)
5. Paste in Supabase (Ctrl+V)
6. Click RUN

You'll see: "Setup Complete! Sign in to your app now."

## Step 2: Sign In (30 seconds)

1. Go to: http://localhost:5173
2. Click "Sign in with Google"
3. Complete Google sign-in

Your profile is automatically created with role='customer'

## Step 3: Make Yourself Admin (30 seconds)

1. Click your avatar in navbar
2. Click "My Profile"
3. Select "Admin" role
4. Click "Save Changes"

Done! Now you can access `/admin`
