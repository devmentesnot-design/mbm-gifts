THE PROBLEM:
Your WHITE logo is INVISIBLE in Google because Google shows it on a 
WHITE/LIGHT background. You need a version with RED background!

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
   ⚡ QUICK SOLUTION - DO THIS NOW:
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1: Create Google/Social Logo (10 minutes)
────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Go to: https://www.canva.com (free, no install needed)

2. Click "Create a design"

3. Choose "Custom size" → Enter: 1200 x 1200 pixels

4. Click on background → Choose color: #8c1119 (your red)
   OR use gold: #FFD700

5. Click "Upload" → Upload your WHITE logo (logo.png)

6. Drag logo to center

7. Resize logo to fill 60-70% of the canvas

8. Optional: Add text "Luxury Gifts Ethiopia" below logo

9. Click "Share" → "Download" → Choose PNG

10. Rename downloaded file to: logo-social.png

11. Put file in: /public/ folder of your project

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 2: Create Favicon (5 minutes)
────────────────────────────────────

1. Open Canva again

2. Create custom size: 64 x 64 pixels

3. Add white background

4. Upload your logo

5. Change logo color to dark red #8c1119
   (In Canva: Click logo → Click color → Enter #8c1119)

6. Download as PNG

7. Rename to: favicon.png

8. Put in: /public/ folder

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 3: Deploy & Test (2 minutes)
────────────────────────────────────

1. Push files to your hosting

2. Go to: https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/

3. Enter: https://www.mbmgifts.app

4. Click "Scrape Again"

5. You should see your logo on RED background! ✅
